## 1. Overview & Innovation Focus

"How Might We" (HMW) questions transform identified user pain points into generative springboards for interface design and technical architecture. The questions below target the six core operational pillars of the FoodBridge platform.

---

## 2. Pillar 1: Easy & Frictionless Donation Creation

- **HMW-01:** How might we make posting 35 kg of hot surplus banquet food faster and simpler than throwing it into a trash bin?
- **HMW-02:** How might we design a donation wizard that captures food preparation time, holding conditions, and allergens in under 90 seconds without feeling like an interrogation?
- **HMW-03:** How might we establish clear clothing condition and cleanliness standards so donors only submit wearable, clean garments?
- **HMW-04:** How might we protect a donor's private residence and telephone number while still ensuring the assigned courier finds the exact gate?
- **HMW-05:** How might we provide donors with absolute peace of mind regarding liability while ensuring receiving organizations retain full inspection authority?

---

## 3. Pillar 2: Quick & Predictable NGO Discovery

- **HMW-06:** How might we present surplus food listings to NGO shelter directors so they can assess portions, storage requirements, and remaining shelf life at a glance?
- **HMW-07:** How might we filter and sort discovery feeds by geographic proximity and urgency so shelters only see donations they can realistically absorb?
- **HMW-08:** How might we enable NGOs to claim partial quantities of large donations so smaller shelters are never excluded?
- **HMW-09:** How might we empower NGOs to decline unsuitable or spoiled items at the doorstep without awkwardness, guilt, or platform penalties?

---

## 4. Pillar 3: Simple & Safe Volunteer Task Acceptance

- **HMW-10:** How might we design the volunteer dispatch interface so that a motorcycle rider can operate it safely with one hand or with gloves in bright sunlight?
- **HMW-11:** How might we guarantee that when a volunteer taps "Accept", the task is exclusively locked to them and will never be claimed by another volunteer?
- **HMW-12:** How might we clearly communicate cargo weight, crate counts, and vehicle suitability upfront so volunteers never accept loads they cannot carry?
- **HMW-13:** How might we provide an instant emergency task surrender button that immediately re-routes the task if a volunteer's vehicle breaks down?

---

## 5. Pillar 4: Reliable Pickup, Transit & Handover Verification

- **HMW-14:** How might we provide volunteers with one-tap access to gate directions and donor phone calls without exposing donor credentials on public feeds?
- **HMW-15:** How might we make physical pickup and delivery confirmations effortless (e.g., single tap with haptic confirmation) so couriers don't have to fiddle with complex forms on the street?
- **HMW-16:** How might we keep food recipients mindful of safe temperature holding limits during transit through visual countdown clocks?

---

## 6. Pillar 5: Real-Time Event Coordination & Sync

- **HMW-17:** How might we synchronize donation and task state changes across donor, NGO, and volunteer screens instantly without requiring manual pull-to-refresh?
- **HMW-18:** How might we deliver timely, non-intrusive push notifications that alert volunteers only to high-urgency rescues in their declared patrol zones?
- **HMW-19:** How might we ensure the mobile app functions reliably and preserves form inputs when moving through basements or weak 3G/4G coverage areas?

---

## 7. Pillar 6: Trust, Verification & Governance

- **HMW-20:** How might we streamline the NGO accreditation workflow so legitimate charities can be verified by administrators in under 15 minutes?
- **HMW-21:** How might we build an immutable, indisputable audit ledger that resolves donor-volunteer disputes with objective facts?
- **HMW-22:** How might we quantify and celebrate community impact (meals served, carbon avoided) while strictly protecting the privacy and dignity of individual beneficiaries?
