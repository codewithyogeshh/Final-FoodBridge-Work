# FoodBridge — Problem Statements & How Might We (HMW) Questions

**Project:** FoodBridge: Real-Time Food and Clothing Donation & Redistribution Platform  
**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Phase:** Phase 2 — Design Thinking & Empathy Mapping  
**Status:** Completed  
**Methodological Note:** These Point of View (POV) problem statements and How Might We (HMW) questions translate insights from the empathy maps and user journeys into actionable innovation challenges.

---

## 1. Point of View (POV) Problem Statements

The POV formula:
$$\text{[User Profile]} \text{ needs a way to } \text{[User Need]} \text{ because } \text{[Surprising Insight / Root Cause]}.$$

### POV 1: The Commercial Food Donor (Ramesh)
> **Ramesh, a busy banquet catering manager, needs a way to** publish surplus food for immediate rescue in under 90 seconds without exposing his personal contact details or fearing legal liabilities, **because** his kitchen closes late at night and staff cannot spend time on tedious forms or managing phone negotiations with unverified strangers.

### POV 2: The Household & Clothing Contributor (Priya)
> **Priya, a conscious urban apartment resident, needs a way to** donate wearable garments and pantry staples with guaranteed privacy and transparent condition criteria, **because** she feels unsafe having her exact apartment address visible on public bulletin boards and wants assurance that her clothes directly help impoverished families rather than being discarded or resold.

### POV 3: The NGO Operations Coordinator (Sunita)
> **Sunita, a dedicated night shelter director, needs a way to** reserve exact portions of wholesome food and clean clothing with dependable transportation assistance, **because** her shelter operates on strict budgets without dedicated delivery vans, and she cannot risk serving compromised or unhygienic meals to vulnerable shelter residents.

### POV 4: The Community Field Volunteer (Rahul)
> **Rahul, a college student operating on a two-wheeler, needs a way to** evaluate and claim pickup tasks with one tap, access precise door-to-door directions, and verify handover seamlessly, **because** he is navigating busy streets, wearing gloves, and handling fragile food containers where any delay or confusion risks food spoilage.

### POV 5: The Platform Administrator (Vikram)
> **Vikram, a trust and safety operations manager, needs a way to** rigorously vet NGO legal certificates and arbitrate logistics disputes in seconds, **because** fraudulent actors or food safety failures would completely destroy the community trust necessary for surplus resource redistribution.

## 2. "How Might We" (HMW) Opportunity Questions

### 2.1 HMW: Donor Friction & Safety
- **HMW-DON-01:** How might we make posting 50 kg of surplus food faster and simpler than dumping it into a garbage bin?
- **HMW-DON-02:** How might we provide donors with absolute peace of mind regarding liability while ensuring receiving organizations retain full inspection authority?
- **HMW-DON-03:** How might we protect a donor's private residence and telephone number while still providing seamless access to the assigned courier?
- **HMW-DON-04:** How might we celebrate and quantify a donor's environmental and social contribution to encourage habitual donation?

### 2.2 HMW: NGO Trust & Allocation
- **HMW-NGO-01:** How might we give NGOs predictable, real-time visibility into incoming food donations so they can plan meals hours ahead?
- **HMW-NGO-02:** How might we guarantee that multiple NGOs never fight over or double-claim the same donation batch?
- **HMW-NGO-03:** How might we empower NGOs to decline unsuitable or spoiled items at the doorstep without awkwardness, guilt, or platform penalties?
- **HMW-NGO-04:** How might we enable NGOs to report their community impact in under 30 seconds without ever collecting or exposing sensitive beneficiary PII?

### 2.3 HMW: Volunteer Field Experience (Special Logistics Focus)
- **HMW-VOL-01:** How might we design the volunteer dispatch interface so that a motorcycle rider can operate it safely with one hand or with gloves?
- **HMW-VOL-02:** How might we guarantee that when a volunteer taps "Accept", the task is exclusively locked to them and will never be claimed by another volunteer?
- **HMW-VOL-03:** How might we clearly communicate cargo weight and container volume upfront so a volunteer never accepts a load they cannot carry?
- **HMW-VOL-04:** How might we provide a seamless "one-touch release" if a volunteer's vehicle breaks down, ensuring the food is re-routed before it spoils?
- **HMW-VOL-05:** How might we give volunteers deep emotional gratification for their physical labor after completing a delivery?

### 2.4 HMW: Governance, Verification & System Integrity
- **HMW-ADM-01:** How might we streamline NGO legal credential verification so genuine charities can be approved in under 15 minutes?
- **HMW-ADM-02:** How might we create an indisputable, immutable audit trail of the entire custody chain to resolve disputes instantly?
- **HMW-ADM-03:** How might we automatically detect and flag suspicious listings or potential resellers before any harm occurs?
