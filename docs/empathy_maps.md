# FoodBridge — Empathy Maps & Stakeholder Analysis

**Project:** FoodBridge: Real-Time Food and Clothing Donation & Redistribution Platform  
**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Phase:** Phase 2 — Design Thinking & Empathy Mapping  
**Status:** Completed  
**Methodological Note:** These empathy maps are synthesized from domain research, NGO operational benchmarks, food recovery field studies, and volunteer logistics observations. All insights are explicitly marked as *Hypotheses & Modelled Assumptions* to be validated during initial pilot rollouts.

---

## 1. Overview of the Empathy Mapping Framework

Empathy maps visualize user attitudes and behaviors across four foundational quadrants:
- **Says:** Direct quotes, explicit statements, or declared expectations.
- **Thinks:** Unspoken internal reflections, doubts, beliefs, and emotional subtext.
- **Does:** Observable physical actions, habits, workarounds, and routines.
- **Feels:** Core emotional states, anxieties, frustrations, and aspirations.
- **Pains & Gains:** Obstacles, fears, and desired positive outcomes.
