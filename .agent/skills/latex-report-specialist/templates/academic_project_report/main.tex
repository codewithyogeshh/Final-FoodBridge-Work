% Abstract
\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}
\thispagestyle{plain}
Food insecurity and commercial food waste remain two of the most critical structural challenges in urban logistics. In urban and peri-urban centers, large quantities of hygienically prepared surplus meals from catering services, restaurants, and educational hostels are discarded daily, while shelters, orphanages, and community feeding programs face acute shortages. 

This project introduces \textbf{FoodBridge}, a cross-platform real-time logistics and verification platform built with Flutter, Supabase, and distributed synchronization. The platform connects commercial donors, non-governmental organizations (NGOs), and community volunteers through an optimistic state engine with cryptographic two-factor handovers (Pickup and Delivery OTP verification). The system guarantees zero unauthorized claims through strict NGO Darpan accreditation gates, automated volunteer routing, and dynamic multi-device synchronization. Empirical testing confirms sub-second end-to-end status propagation and high user task efficiency.
